Removing backgrounds from photos is essential for product photography, profile pictures, and creative design. Lumea runs subject segmentation directly in your browser.

### How It Works

Lumea uses the MediaPipe Selfie Segmentation model to identify subjects in your photos. Calculations run on your device GPU or CPU without sending photos to remote servers.

### How to Use

1. **Upload your image**: Drop a photo (JPG, PNG, WebP) into the tool.
2. **Subject Detection**: The model detects the primary subject and isolates it from the background.
3. **Refine**: Adjust edge smoothing and refinement intensity for clean edges.
4. **Download**: Save your result as a transparent PNG.

### Why Choose Lumea

- **Direct local processing**: Photos never leave your computer. Segmentation calculations execute on local hardware.
- **High quality**: Download transparent PNGs ready for design work.
- **No limits**: Free to use without accounts or processing quotas.
